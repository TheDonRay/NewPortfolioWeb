# Surface: portfolio home (single page)

Scope: whole single-page site, src/App.jsx and all sections. Visitor mode: Experience leaning Persuade (portfolio; action = book a call / email / resume).
Audience: recruiters and eng managers, and freelance clients, equally. Constraints: keep every function (live GitHub projects carousel with dots/arrows/keys/swipe, Cal.com inline embed with fallback, nav active tracking, resume, legacy redirects). Direction pinned by the user's references in reference/ (blend of all three); build path code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A night stage for a backend engineer: the page is a dark broadcast frame where a live signal field runs behind enormous split uppercase type. Refuses the white card-grid portfolio and the centred headline-over-cards hero.

OWN-WORLD: True black stage with graphite panels (#000, #111317, #181b20), ink #f2f3f5, mute #9aa0a8, one signal blue #3d7bff carried by the field's sparks, the status dot, focus and selection. One family, Geist: uppercase medium display with tight tracking, small quiet sentence-case UI. Hairlines at 12% white, pills fully rounded, panels 20px radius.

STORY: Visitor sees the name and role at poster scale, understands "backend engineer in New York, available", scans real work and experience, then books a call or emails without hunting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height canvas field (dotted flow lines bending around an elliptical lens, blue sparks). "RAYAT" at top-left and "CHOWDHURY" right-aligned on the line below, together ~70% of width; role caption under RAYAT at left. Bottom band: intro paragraph left, two pill actions right (See my work primary, Read my resume). Nav top: monogram + name left, links centre, "Book a call" pill with blue dot right.

FORM: Pinned by the user, no seed roll (no seed key): the user wrote "The references are in the /reference folder for how I want the site to look" and confirmed the hero should "Blend all three" (cinematic split headline + Arkitek refined UI + DeepBook signal field); a pinned direction beats the roll. Signature interaction: pointer parts the field. Motion: one load sequence (headline lines rise out of a mask, field fades in).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
